Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-22 · effective October 22, 2026

EdgePro is a free research app. This page explains what it stores and how to remove it.

Without an account

Your tracked bets, limits, favorites and graded Desk picks stay in your browser (localStorage and IndexedDB). Nothing is sent to us unless you turn on an option below.

Optional sharing

  • Anonymous grade sharing (Record page, off by default): sends graded results only, with a random device id, no account.
  • Community splits (on by default, can be turned off in Tools): when you track a moneyline, spread or total, the market and side are sent with a random device id. No amount is shared.

With an account

If you create an account we store your email (for sign-in), your synced bets and graded picks, and your profile: username, display name, bio, avatar image, favorite teams and sports, and whether the profile is public. Picks you post to your profile, follows, and the record computed from them are stored too. Public profiles and their picks can be seen by anyone; private profiles are hidden from everyone but you.

Who processes data

Accounts and data are hosted by Supabase (US East). Scores and stats come from ESPN's public API, prices from Polymarket and Kalshi public APIs and The Odds API (through our server, without your data). We don't sell data, run ads or use trackers.

Your controls

  • Export everything: Tools → Account → Export (JSON or CSV).
  • Delete your account and all cloud data: Tools → Account → Delete account. This removes your profile, picks, follows, synced data and avatar.
  • Clear local data: your browser's site-data settings.

Age

EdgePro is for adults 21 and over where sports wagering is legal.
